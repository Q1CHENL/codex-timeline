About

The Codex desktop home screen across its public app versions and model changes, rendered from each official package’s original frontend with empty sample data. Stop on any version to hover its controls, open its menus, toggle its sidebar and panels, or type in the composer; nothing is sent.

Dates are package archive times. Model labels are reconstructed from official release notes and model catalogs. Account features, saved settings and server rollouts are not included, so a real account may have looked different.
